Offline spell-check word list - assets/dictionary/en-us.txt
============================================================

Source 1 - zxcvbn english_wikipedia frequency list
---------------------------------------------------
The base word list is derived from the `english_wikipedia.txt` frequency
list shipped with the zxcvbn password-strength library (Dropbox, Inc.).

  Project: https://github.com/dropbox/zxcvbn
  File:    data/english_wikipedia.txt (as bundled in Chromium ZxcvbnData)

The source list was normalised for this product: lowercased, restricted to
words of two or more ASCII letters, de-duplicated and sorted, one word per
line (UTF-8, LF).

Source 2 - SCOWL en_US hunspell dictionary (DEF-479 supplement)
---------------------------------------------------------------
To fix DEF-479 (common everyday words missing from the base frequency
list) the list was UNIONED with the SCOWL-derived en_US hunspell
dictionary. Stem entries were lowercased, stripped of hunspell affix flags
and morphological fields, restricted to ASCII letters, then merged into the
existing list. No existing words were removed; the merged result is
de-duplicated and sorted (UTF-8, LF).

  Name:    SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists), en_US, SCOWL size 60
  Project: https://github.com/kevina/wordlist
  Site:    http://wordlist.sourceforge.net
